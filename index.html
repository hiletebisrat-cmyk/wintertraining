Winter Dance Intensive: 7 Weeks, 7 Teams

Solo training curriculum built from the full IDL 2026 season and the UNLV Rebel Girls and Company championship years. One team per week. Learn every competitive set, drill it, film it.

Weeks 1 through 6 map each IDL team's 2026 season event by event. Week 7 is the finale: the UNLV hip hop routines from 2022 through 2026, UDA Nationals versions paired with ICU Worlds versions.

Week 1: Brotherhood Week 2: GRV Week 3: Royal Family Week 4: 1MILLION Week 5: Jam Republic Week 6: Quick Style Week 7: UNLV Finale
How to read this page. Every routine card shows the event, the round, the result, and the songs. Where a routine has video, each camera view is embedded below it with a link to watch on the original platform. Routines marked no video found have no public individual cut; use the full event replay links where listed, or train the choreography from the closest available set. Songs marked UNCERTAIN could not be verified from available sources.

Your former team. You danced with the Rebel Girls and Company from April 2023 to May 2026, winning three national championships and a Team USA gold at the ICU World Championship. This week studies the team's ICU Worlds hip hop routines paired with the UDA College Nationals routines they were built from.

Key fact: UNLV has exactly three ICU Worlds hip hop routines (2022, 2023, 2025), and all three won gold. No Worlds routine exists for 2024 or 2026. Training days: Day 1 and 2, the 2025 pair.
